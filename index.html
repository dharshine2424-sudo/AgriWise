🤝 Cooperative Selling

Join with other farmers to sell together, share costs, and earn more money.

🤝

What is Cooperative Selling?

When multiple farmers combine their produce and sell together as a group, they get better prices, save on transport, and have stronger bargaining power with buyers. This is called Cooperative Selling. It is one of the most effective ways for small farmers to increase their income.

💰

Better Prices

Large bulk quantities attract buyers who pay 10–20% more per kg than individual small sellers.

🚛

Shared Transport

Split vehicle costs with other farmers. 5 farmers sharing a truck saves ₹500–₹2,000 per trip.

📦

Bulk Packaging

Buy packaging materials at wholesale rates — save 30–40% compared to buying individually.

🏦

Better Loans

Farmer cooperatives can access government schemes and low-interest loans more easily.

📢

More Bargaining Power

A group of 10 farmers can negotiate better terms than each farmer selling alone.

🛒

Direct to Buyers

Cooperatives can sell directly to supermarkets and hotels, bypassing middlemen entirely.

Solo vs. Cooperative Selling — Example with Tomato

Factor Solo Farmer 🤝 Cooperative Group (5 Farmers)
Quantity 100 kg Tomato 500 kg (5 farmers × 100 kg)

Gross Revenue ₹1,800 ₹12,000 (÷ 5 = ₹2,400 per farmer)
Transport Cost ₹240 (alone, 15 km × ₹8 × 2) ₹48 per farmer (shared — ₹240 ÷ 5)
Packaging ₹50 ₹35 per farmer (bulk rate)
Commission (3%) ₹54 ₹72 per farmer
Net Profit per Farmer ₹1,456 ₹2,245  (+54% more!)
